# Visual Storyboard

## 0 Cover — magazine opener

- Copy: BACK TO SCHOOL / 状态，慢慢回来 / 新学期开学心理调适指南
- Layout: warm-cream masthead, narrow blue label, large campus photo, small date stamp and loading dots; HTML title remains primary; transparent handwritten art is secondary.
- Palette: cream `#F7F3E9`, sky `#6FA6D8`, sun `#F1C75B`, ink `#26364A`.
- Assets: A01 cover campus photo; A02 title art.
- Transition: photo baseline ends in a thin hand-drawn blue wave.

## 1 Switching back — offset photo collage

- Copy: three short observations and the normalization line.
- Layout: one wide photo, then two unequal photos aligned opposite each other with text slipping between; safe-flow margins mimic collage without absolute positioning.
- Palette: warm paper, faded amber, muted denim.
- Assets: A03 desk; A04 tree-shadow walk; A05 corridor/classroom.
- Motifs: LOADING..., paper tape, small sun.
- Transition: a four-dot loading meter becomes four mood colors.

## 2 Mood check — varied 2×2 editorial cards

- Copy: four non-diagnostic states; one-line descriptions; closing principle.
- Layout: table-based two columns; cards vary in background, border, illustration scale, and label treatment.
- Palette: expectation yellow, loading blue, anxious gray-blue, quiet sage.
- Assets: A06–A09 four square youth editorial illustrations.
- Motifs: weather mark and small dots; no test scoring.
- Transition: card colors converge into a cool blue-gray section header.

## 3 Re-sync — rational magazine infographic

- Copy: body rhythm, task mode, relationship mode; one large conclusion.
- Layout: central illustration followed by three compact numbered labels; oversized sentence on a blue-gray paper panel.
- Palette: `#E8EEF2`, `#B7C8D3`, deep blue ink.
- Assets: A10 re-sync illustration.
- Motifs: clock tick marks, curved arrow.
- Transition: curved arrow warms from blue to apricot, introducing SOFT RESET.

## 4 Soft reset — four deliberately different constructions

- Sleep: rounded cream paper card with transparent moon/alarm decoration (A11).
- Task: scrapbook TODAY LIST using stationery collage (A12); HTML check list.
- Emotion: full-width cinematic dusk campus photograph with the sentence in a separate high-contrast block (A13).
- Connection: minimal line illustration beside a speech-bubble text panel (A14).
- Palette: night blue → apricot → amber dusk → sage.
- Transition: pencil line from the connection block points into the save card.

## 5 Buffer card — screenshot module

- Copy: four fill-in prompts and screenshot note.
- Layout: centered paper card with generous writing lines, clipped-corner feel using borders; decorations remain independent PNGs.
- Palette: paper `#F2E8D5`, sage `#A8BCA3`, dusty rose `#D9B8B2`, graphite.
- Assets: A15 pencil; A16 tape; A17 leaf; A18 headphones.
- Motifs: paper clip, tiny star, date stamp.
- Transition: reduced ornament and extra whitespace.

## 6 Ending — visual deceleration

- Copy: permission to seek help; persistence/impact safety sentence; pointer to center.
- Layout: quiet near-full-width campus-sky photo, one calm cream text panel, then a reserved poster slot.
- Palette: dusk sky blue with a warm rim of sunset.
- Assets: A19 ending sky photo.
- Poster: placeholder comment and bordered 750px-safe block; no institution details fabricated.
